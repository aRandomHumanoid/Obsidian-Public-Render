# Link with alias

An alias that differs from both the target's filename and its title survives
as plain text: [[internal-roadmap|the thing we discussed]].

An alias equal to the target's filename does not: [[internal-roadmap|internal-roadmap]].

An alias equal to the target's title does not either:
[[internal-roadmap|Q4 acquisition targets]].

Case and surrounding whitespace are not an escape hatch:
[[internal-roadmap|  q4 ACQUISITION targets ]].
